# Onboarding Template

Purpose: help a new maintainer understand the system quickly.

Tone: navigational, factual, evidence-linked.

Required outputs:

- `wiki/首页.md`
- `wiki/01-系统全景.md`
- `wiki/02-现状架构.md`
- `wiki/03-关键业务链路.md`
- `wiki/04-风险与技术债.md`
- `wiki/05-决策与待办.md`

Rules:

- 首页 is navigation, not a long report.
- Every page links to evidence YAML.
- Unknown owners, missing docs, and failed analysis are explicit.
- Do not hide risk for onboarding friendliness.
