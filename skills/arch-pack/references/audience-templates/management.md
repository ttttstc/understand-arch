# Management Template

Purpose: communicate risk, business impact, decision ask, and resource needs.

Required sections:

- One-page executive summary.
- Business goal.
- Decision or approval needed.
- Risk if doing nothing.
- Recommended path and why.
- Timeline shape.
- Resource or dependency ask.
- Evidence appendix.

Rules:

- Use business language.
- Avoid unexplained technical acronyms.
- Keep summary to one page or less.
- Do not invent quantified impact; cite source or mark as estimate.
