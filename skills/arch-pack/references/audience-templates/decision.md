# Decision Template

Purpose: help a decision maker choose, fund, defer, or reject an architecture direction.

Required sections:

- One-page summary.
- Decision needed.
- Recommended option.
- Alternatives rejected.
- Top risks and mitigations.
- Cost/effort shape.
- Org KB violations or approvals required.
- Evidence links.

Rules:

- Put the decision ask near the top.
- Avoid implementation detail unless it changes risk or cost.
- Every number or recommendation links to source artifacts.
- If evidence is incomplete, say what decision cannot be made yet.
